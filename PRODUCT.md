# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Mentee (Andi):** ~22, university student or fresh graduate. Needs specific mentorship for real projects — not generic tutorials. Budget-conscious (Rp 100k–300k/session). Their job: find someone who can dive into their specific problem and guide them through it.

**Mentor (Budi):** ~28, professional practitioner (engineer, designer, data). Wants to teach and earn without wasting time on unserious students. Their job: find motivated learners matching their expertise and offer a competitive proposal.

**Context:** Indonesian market, bilingual platform (English primary with Indonesian i18n). Users access via mobile and desktop, with desktop prioritized for form-heavy flows (request creation, proposal writing).

## Product Purpose

A C2C marketplace connecting people who need specific skill mentorship (Mentees) with verified practitioners who can teach them (Mentors). Unlike course platforms that broadcast generic content, Ajarin matches real human need to real human expertise through a transparent proposal/bidding mechanism. Success is a high match rate, fast time-to-match, and repeat usage from both sides.

## Brand Personality

**Specific, Earned, Direct.**

The platform values specificity over generality — detailed requests attract better proposals. Trust is earned through real matches and reviews, not badges or streaks. Communication is direct and clear, not playful or gamified.

## Anti-references

- **Generic course platforms (Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare).** Ajarin is not a content library. It's a matching platform. No video thumbnails, no course catalogs, no "enroll now" patterns.
- **Social media / engagement feeds.** This is a utility, not a feed. No infinite scroll, no engagement-maximizing algorithms, no notifications designed for addiction.
- **Over-gamified gig platforms (Fiverr, Upwork badge systems).** Reputation is earned through completed matches and reviews. No badges, no levels, no "Top Rated" gamification.
- **Tinder-swipe matching mechanics.** Matching is a deliberate decision after reviewing proposals, not a quick binary choice.

## Design Principles

1. **Specificity over volume.** Forms, proposals, and profiles reward detail. A good match starts with a good description. Design surfaces that encourage thorough input without feeling like homework.

2. **Trust through transparency.** Mentees see all proposals. Mentors see all relevant requests. No hidden information, no dark patterns. The interface surfaces what each side needs to decide confidently.

3. **Two-sided clarity.** The UI always knows which role the user is in and surfaces what matters to them. A Mentee's dashboard shows requests and proposal counts. A Mentor's dashboard shows relevant requests and proposal statuses. Never confuse the two.

4. **Earned, not gamified.** Reputation comes from real matches and verified reviews. No streaks, badges, or achievement systems. Visual hierarchy reflects substantive signals (rating, review count, match history) not decorative ones.

5. **Professional warmth.** Competent and helpful — not cold corporate, not playful casual. The tone of a knowledgeable colleague who respects your time. Clear copy, purposeful spacing, confident color use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance for all core interactions.
- Fully responsive from 360px mobile viewport.
- Supports reduced motion preferences.
- Bilingual support (English primary + Indonesian) via i18n.
- Color palette meets contrast requirements; not exclusively reliant on color for state changes.
